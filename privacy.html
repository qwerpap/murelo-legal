Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026.

Murelo ("the app") is a wall pilates program. We built it to work without an account and to keep your data on your device.

What stays on your device

Your quiz answers (goal, level, areas to go easy on, minutes per day, reminder time) and your workout history (which days you finished and for how long) are stored only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use Adapty (adapty.io) to manage subscription status and paywall prices. Adapty receives an anonymous app-specific profile identifier, purchase and subscription events, basic device information (model, OS version, app version, country) and, if you agree to share them in the quiz, your answers to the goal/level/minutes questions to choose which paywall to show. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or health data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local daily reminder on your device. No push server is involved.

Children

Murelo is not directed at children under 13.

Health

Murelo does not provide medical advice. Consult your doctor before beginning any exercise program.
