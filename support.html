Support

Need help with Murelo? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com — we answer within two working days.

Restore a subscription

Open the Me tab and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Cancel a subscription

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Murelo → Cancel Subscription.

Reminders don't arrive

Settings → Notifications → Murelo → Allow Notifications, then pick a time in the Me tab.

Safety

Stop any move that hurts. If you have knee, lower-back or wrist concerns, choose them in the quiz and Murelo leaves out moves that load them.
